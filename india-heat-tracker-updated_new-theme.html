About this graphic: Departures are calculated as latest reported maximum temperature minus the historical normal for that date, per the uploaded workbook. One representative city stands in for each state or union territory on the map; cities sharing a state with the representative (for example Srinagar and Leh alongside Jammu for Jammu & Kashmir, or Ahmedabad alongside Gandhinagar for Gujarat) appear in the table and chart but do not separately shade the map. State boundaries follow the pre-2014/2019 administrative map bundled with this tool, so Hyderabad is grouped with Andhra Pradesh and there is no separate Telangana; "Uttaranchal" and "Orissa" retain their earlier names.
